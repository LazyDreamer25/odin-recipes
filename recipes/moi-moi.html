Moi-Moi

Photo by: Bukky658 / CC BY -SA 4.0

Description

pudding made from a savory blend of peeled black-eyed peas or brown beans, onions, and fresh peppers. It is seasoned with crayfish, oil, and spices, and often includes rich additions like hard-boiled eggs, fish, or corned beef. The mixture is wrapped in traditional leaves, foil, or small cups and steamed until it sets into a soft, savory cake

Ingredient

  • Beans: 3 cups brown beans or black-eyed peas
  • Peppers: 1 large red bell pepper (tatashe) and 2-3 scotch bonnets (ata rodo), to taste
  • Onions: 1 large bulb
  • Oil: \(\frac{1}{2}\) cup vegetable oil or palm oil (or a mix)
  • Seasoning: 2-3 bouillon cubes, salt to taste, and 1-2 tablespoons ground crayfish
  • Liquid: 1 to 2 cups of warm water or fish/meat stock
  • Optional Toppings: Hard-boiled eggs (sliced), deboned mackerel (titus), or corned beef

Steps

  1. Prep the Beans
    • Soak the dry beans in water for about 10–15 minutes.
    • Rub the beans between your hands or pulse briefly in a blender to loosen the outer skins.
    • Submerge in water and carefully pour off the floating skins. Repeat until the beans are completely peeled and clean.
  2. Blend the Mixture
    • Add the peeled beans, chopped onions, bell peppers, and scotch bonnets to your blender.
    • Add enough water to help the blades run smoothly and blend until the paste is completely smooth and grit-free.
  3. Mix and Season
    • Pour the thick bean batter into a large mixing bowl.
    • Stir in your oil, crayfish, bouillon cubes, and salt.
    • Add warm water or stock to achieve a moderately thick, pourable consistency.
    • Whisk vigorously to incorporate air, which yields a fluffier, softer texture.
  4. Pour and Add Toppings
    • Grease your containers (traditional Moi Moi leaves, banana leaves, ramekins, or small aluminum foil dishes).
    • Scoop the batter into your wrappers, leaving a little room for expansion.
    • Add your preferred toppings directly into the batter—such as a piece of deboned fish, a slice of hard-boiled egg, or a spoonful of corned beef. Seal the packages tightly.
  5. Cook
    • Bring a pot of water to a boil. Create a base or rack at the bottom of the pot (you can use foil or extra clean leaf stems).
    • Arrange your wrapped or cupped Moi Moi on the rack.
    • Cover the pot tightly, reduce the heat to medium, and steam for about 45 to 60 minutes. Add boiling water occasionally if the level drops.
    • Remove from the heat and allow to cool for 15-30 minutes so the batter fully solidifies before serving.
