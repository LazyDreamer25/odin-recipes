Melon soup

Melon soup (Egusi) and swallow ( Nigerian dish).jpgby Beendy234is licensed under CC-BY-SA-4.0

Description

Melon Soup, popularly known as Egusi Soup, is a rich and savory Nigerian classic made from ground melon seeds, palm oil, leafy vegetables, and a variety of meats and seafood. It is one of the most celebrated dishes across Nigeria, loved for its unique, creamy texture and deep umami flavor. The soup is traditionally served hot alongside swallows like pounded yam, eba, or fufu, or paired with white rice.

Ingredient

  • The Star: 2 cups ground Egusi (melon seeds)
  • The Base: \(\frac{1}{2}\) cup palm oil and 1 large onion (finely chopped)
  • The Greens: 2 cups chopped fresh pumpkin leaves (ugu), spinach, or bitter leaf
  • Proteins: 500g assorted meats (beef, shaki, or kpomo) and 1 medium smoked or dry fish
  • Flavorings: 3 tablespoons ground crayfish, 2-3 blended scotch bonnets (ata rodo), and 1 tablespoon locust beans (iru)
  • Seasoning: 2 seasoning cubes and salt to taste

Steps

  1. Prep the Proteins
  2. Season your assorted meats with a handful of sliced onions, pepper, and seasoning cubes.
  3. Boil until tender, then separate the meat from the rich meat broth (stock). Keep the stock.
  4. Debone and break your smoked fish into bite-sized pieces, washing it thoroughly.
  5. Create the Egusi Paste
  6. Pour your ground melon seeds into a bowl.
  7. Add a few tablespoons of warm water or meat stock.
  8. Mix thoroughly until it forms a thick, smooth, dough-like paste, then set aside.
  9. Fry the Base
  10. Heat your palm oil in a pot on medium heat for about 2 minutes (do not let it smoke).
  11. Toss in the chopped onions and locust beans (iru), frying for 2 to 3 minutes until fragrant.
  12. Scoop small lumps of the Egusi paste into the hot oil using your hands or a spoon.
  13. Fry on medium-low heat for 10 minutes, stirring gently so it forms small, solid curd-like chunks.
  14. Simmer with Stock
  15. Pour in your saved meat stock and the blended scotch bonnets.
  16. Add your cooked meats, dried prawns, ground crayfish, and smoked fish pieces.
  17. Cover the pot tightly and let it simmer on medium heat for 15 minutes until the oil floats to the top.
  18. Add Vegetables and Serve
  19. Taste the soup and adjust with extra salt or seasoning cubes if needed.
  20. Stir in your chopped pumpkin leaves, spinach, or washed bitter leaf.
  21. Simmer uncovered for 3 to 5 minutes so the vegetables stay bright green and tender, then remove from heat
